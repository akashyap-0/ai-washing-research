CODEBOOK AMENDMENT v1.1.0 (supersedes v1.0.0 on this point only)

Rule 4a — Named AI systems.

The following named products, systems and platforms are AI systems for purposes of
this codebook. Where a passage names one, the passage HAS AI content, whether or not
it also uses the words "AI", "artificial intelligence", "machine learning" or similar:

  Agentforce; Autopilot; Full Self-Driving / FSD; Copilot / Copilot+ / Copilot Pro;
  watsonx; Oracle Autonomous Database; Amazon Sparrow; Amazon Robin; Amazon Cardinal;
  Amazon Proteus; Amazon Kendra; Amazon Bedrock; AWS Trainium; AWS Inferentia;
  CodeWhisperer; Amazon Q; NVIDIA DRIVE; AI Cockpit; Kensho; ambient intelligence.

This list is closed and fixed in advance. Do not extend it by inference at label time,
and do not treat any other brand name as AI content.

Establishing AI content is NOT a label. Rules 3 and 4 continue to apply in full: a
passage may name one of these systems and still receive all zeros if it asserts no
opportunity, risk, efficiency, adoption, augmentation, marketing or employment claim
about it. A name appearing in a segment-composition list, a safe-harbor product
enumeration, or an expense footnote justifies nothing on its own.

Rationale: v1.0.0 made AI content contingent on a firm's choice of vocabulary. Firms
use the category word ("AI") in legal-defensive prose and brand names in promotional
prose, so the v1.0.0 rule blanked AI content asymmetrically by register and by firm.
That error is correlated with the construct under study.
