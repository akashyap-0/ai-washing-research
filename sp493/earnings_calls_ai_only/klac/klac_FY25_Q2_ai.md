## Prepared remarks

### Richard P. Wallace, President, Chief Executive Officer, and Director
1. KLA's outperformance for the year was driven by a return to growth at the leading edge, which includes increased investments in AI, high-performance computing and continued momentum in advanced packaging, as well as sustainable performance for KLA's services business.
2. Across all sectors, there are technology development investments supporting AI and HBM, as well as a strengthening supply/demand environment which positions the wafer fab equipment industry for growth in calendar 2025.
3. AI continues to be a crucial catalyst for KLA.
4. We are well aware of the recent revelations of DeepSeek and the implications that it portends to diminish demand for advanced semiconductor in support of the AI infrastructure build-out.
5. As a company that has been developing AI models for use in our own inspection systems for many years, our own experience supports the theory that increased compute efficiency enables more adoption of AI on our platforms.
6. We see no reason to believe that the increased compute efficiency in AI will have an impact on the advanced demand environment in the foreseeable future.
7. AI is both an important driver and enabler of KLA's business.
8. Specific drivers connected to AI that are a positive for KLA's growth are higher volume and higher value wafer demand, more complex designs, accelerating product cycles, larger die size, and growing advanced packaging demand.

### Bren D. Higgins, Executive Vice President, Chief Financial Officer
9. Growth in calendar 2025 is expected to be fueled principally by increasing investment in both leading-edge foundry/logic and memory to support growing AI and premium mobile demand, offset by lower overall demand from China due to the digestion of elevated levels of investment over the past couple of years.
10. Foundry/logic revenue from semiconductor customers is forecasted to be approximately 73%, and memory is expected to be approximately 27% of semi-process control systems revenue to semiconductor customers. ⚑ uncertain
11. Other model assumptions include non-GAAP other income and expense net of approximately $36 million expense for the March quarter and expect this to be roughly consistent throughout the calendar year. ⚑ uncertain
