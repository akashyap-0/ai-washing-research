## Q&A

### William David Mosley, Chief Executive Officer
1. We attribute the underlying demand drivers to an increase from both traditional cloud computing workloads, as well as new AI-related deployments.
2. While cloud service providers are continuing to build out that infrastructure, they increasingly focus on developing, deploying, and monetizing AI applications.
3. This involves both training of large language models and expanding the entire hardware stack to support future generative AI content-driven growth. ⚑ context-dependent
4. While HDD demand pull-through related to AI is still relatively small, we believe HDDs will play a crucial role in enabling both of these phases of the AI adoption curve.
5. By offering cost-efficient, scalable storage solutions, HDDs are ideal for maintaining the integrity of AI training data sets, as well as preserving the valuable content that AI engines are projected to generate in the future.
6. We've also started to see incremental demand for higher-density storage-specific solutions, due in part to enterprises putting storage capacity in place, either on-prem or in private clouds, as they prepare for future AI applications.
7. This capability is enabling Seagate to deliver significant cost benefits to our customers, as well as offer power and space advantages that are particularly valuable for data center operators as they expand cloud and AI infrastructure. ⚑ context-dependent
8. We are hearing from customers directly, including CSPs, that HDDs already play a crucial role in extracting value from data in the early stages of AI application deployment.
9. As GenAI engines mature, customers expect an acceleration in content creation that will lead to significant demand for mass capacity storage.
10. We recognize that GenAI is still in its early stages of adoption.
