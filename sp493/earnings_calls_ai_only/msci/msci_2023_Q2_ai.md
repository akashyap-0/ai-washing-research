## Prepared remarks

### Baer Pettit, President and Chief Operating Officer
1. Generative AI and large language models are a big part of that.
2. We've been using AI and natural language processing for a decade now to enhance our content.
3. In ESG research, for example, AI helps us analyze and update more than 7 million data points per month with inputs from more than 4,700 news sources, 150 alternative data sources, 12,000 corporate websites, and submissions from more than 5,200 corporate issuers.
4. We have also used generative AI and LLMs to help us identify, extract, and validate EU taxonomy data four times faster than our previous capabilities.
5. In analytics, we use natural language processing in deep learning in our models to identify securities with similar characteristics to examine emerging industries and to support quantitative investing and systematic alpha use cases.
6. We're also deploying AI on our corporate functions such as finance to help automate contract review, extract information, and process invoices.
7. MSCI is embracing generative AI to enhance the client experience and operate more efficiently.
8. In particular, we are leveraging AI to continue evolving our data processing, audit, and quality mechanisms.
9. In addition, we are working to integrate AI into our MSCI One platform to proactively deliver actionable insights for client portfolios.
