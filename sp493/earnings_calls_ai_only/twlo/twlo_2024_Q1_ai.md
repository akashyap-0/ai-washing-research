## Prepared remarks

### Khozema Shipchandler, Chief Executive Officer
1. Across the board, we're innovating and releasing new products, many of which are underpinned by customer AI, our predictive and generative AI layer.
2. We made progress on a number of our AI products and are driving better synergies with our communications and segment products.
3. In Q1, we announced Agent Copilot, our first of three launches in 2024, where Twilio will natively embed segment into Twilio's communications products.
4. With Agent Copilot, we've embedded unified profiles powered by segment within Flex, giving agents deeper insights into their customers' behaviors and preferences.
5. By accessing the real-time data from unified profiles, Agent Copilot assists in intelligent routing to agents and provides them with actionable insights for each customer interaction, automating and enhancing agent productivity while reducing resolution times.
6. Agent Copilot and unified profiles are currently in public beta and customers, like UniversidadUk, are already leveraging these capabilities within their contact centers.
7. And by using our embedded AI automation tools, they've been able to deflect 70% of support cases in just 2 months.
8. Our customers also continue to realize tangible benefits when using our other customer AI innovations, including voice intelligence, which has gotten a great response from our initial public beta customers.
9. We also recently introduced language operators that uses generative AI and large language models to determine the best sentiment for the overall conversation.
10. And we're continuing to embed AI capabilities into our verification products.
11. We will continue to focus on rationalizing our investments to rightsize segment's cost base, accelerating time to value for customers by using AI to automate onboarding, and enhancing data warehouse interoperability, delivering three products in 2024 that natively embed segment into communications and capitalizing on customer AI momentum.
12. And as mentioned earlier, we delivered focused product innovation like Agent Copilot that demonstrates segment's value when it's natively embedded into communications.
13. Customer AI predictions is continuing to get adopted by new customers.
14. For instance, XP Inc., a Brazilian investment management company, said that since implementing customer AI predictions and by using our out-of-the-box tools, they've been able to save their team 4 weeks of data science work and improved audience engagement and conversion rates.
