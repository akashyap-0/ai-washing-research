## Prepared remarks

### Sarah M. London, Chief Executive Officer
1. Centene demonstrated our thought leadership in marketplace earlier this year by implementing an agent of record lock policy, which was subsequently implemented marketwide by CMS in July. ⚑ uncertain
2. This quarter, we advanced work on a project some of you have heard me speak about, namely the use of AI to automate and optimize our management of provider contracts.
