## Prepared remarks

### Douglas L. Peterson, President and Chief Executive Officer
1. In addition to our strong financial results, we continue to invest in innovation, particularly around generative AI and our strategic initiatives, which we'll touch on shortly.
2. Now, turning to our latest initiatives around Generative AI.
3. Each quarter, we've been highlighting just a few of the many ways we've been leveraging both traditional AI and generative AI within S&P Global.
4. Within Market Intelligence, we introduced new advanced analytics solutions and GenAI functionality through Cap IQ Pro, which allows users to build predictive models, automate workflows and better identify patterns and data more quickly.
5. Within Commodity Insights, we enabled cloud delivery of AI-ready data on clean energy technology.
6. This is a great example of our efforts to make sure that the investments we've made to prepare data sets for ingestion in AI models directly translate into benefits for our customers and economics for our business. ⚑ context-dependent
7. Also, in Commodity Insights, we introduced our AI-powered chatbot called Chat AI, to improve the user experience, answer customer questions in real time and help users more quickly discover the valuable insights in our Platts Connect platform.
8. Within Mobility, we're leveraging GenAI and animation automation to embed the CARFAX Car Fox directly within the vehicle history reports that customers use.
9. Through our recently launched Spark AI Academy, our employees received meaningful training on prompt engineering and other insights into how they can best leverage AI in their own daily work.
10. We're confident that AI will be a strong influence to efficiency, productivity the quality of life for years to come and that, over time, you will see these improvements in our growth and profitability.

### Martina Cheung, President, S&P Global Ratings
