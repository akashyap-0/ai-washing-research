## Prepared remarks

### David C. Kimbell, Chief Executive Officer
1. This quarter, we enhanced our suite of Virtual Try-On and AI-enabled skin and hair analysis experiences with the launch of GLAMlab 2.0, which includes a new 3D engine to enhance precision and stability, shoppable makeup looks, and a new user interface that includes sharing capabilities.
2. We recently partnered with e-commerce tech company, Rokt, to introduce AI non-endemic ads for products and services outside the beauty category, and we are partnering with Roblox, the ultimate virtual universe to create innovative advertising opportunities for our partners.
