## Prepared remarks

### Cristiano Amon, President and Chief Executive Officer
1. Our next-generation Snapdragon Compute platform, with custom Orion CPUs and industry-leading AI acceleration, is on schedule to enable commercial device launches in 2024.
2. I would now like to provide a perspective on the disruptive trends in the artificial intelligence space and the significant opportunity for Qualcomm.
3. Demand for generative artificial intelligence models is growing at an exponential rate.
4. Generative AI models such as ChatGPT, Stable Diffusion, and DALL-E have already scaled to millions of users in a short period of time.
5. We believe that these models will evolve quickly, continue to grow in popularity, and change user experiences across mobile, personal computing, and automotive. ⚑ uncertain
6. Beyond changing Internet search, these models will have an impact on content creation such as text, images, audio, and video for both entertainment and productivity. ⚑ uncertain
7. For these models to realize their full potential and scale, they will need to run locally on devices at the edge. ⚑ uncertain
8. At Mobile World Congress, we demonstrated the world's first on-device Stable Diffusion, a greater than 1 billion parameter foundational model for text-to-image applications running completely on a Snapdragon-powered Android smartphone.
9. In the coming months, we will see significantly improve performance and be able to run models in excess of 10 billion parameters locally on the device, and we will increase this capability substantially for our products in 2024.
10. Qualcomm is uniquely positioned to enable the proliferation of AI use cases on edge devices.
11. We're advancing AI to make core on-device capabilities ubiquitous such as perception, reasoning, action, and now, content creation.
12. With millions of AI-enabled platform shipments per year, unparalleled AI processing performance per watt in the broadest range of device categories from smartphones to PCs, automotive, and IoT, Qualcomm is firmly at the forefront of this upcoming transformation.
13. Further, very large AI models are placing significant incremental demands on energy-intensive and expensive cloud computing infrastructure.
14. As such, a hybrid AI architecture leveraging accelerating computing at the edge can offload or support cloud processing by running AI inferencing directly on the device.
15. Beyond cost optimization, additional benefits of running generative AI on device include improved latency, security, privacy, and the ability to meet data compliance requirements.
