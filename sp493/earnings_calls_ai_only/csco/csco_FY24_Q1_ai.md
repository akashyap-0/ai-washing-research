## Prepared remarks

### Chuck Robbins, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer
1. The combination of Cisco and Splunk will create an end-to-end data platform to enhance our customers' digital resiliency with our complementary capabilities in AI, security and observability.
2. As I previously mentioned, we delivered strong revenues in Q1, which was broad-based across our product portfolio and driven by our customers' investments in generative AI, cloud, security, and full-stack observability.
3. In web scale, we see continued momentum in AI with three of the top 4 customers deploying our hyperscale Ethernet AI fabric.
4. We also already have line of sight to over $1 billion in orders for AI infrastructure from major cloud providers in fiscal year '25.
5. To help advance AI, we are working with key GPU and storage partners to create solutions, including Ethernet technologies, GPU-enabled infrastructure, and joint-tested and validated reference architectures with a commitment to open networking for AI.
6. Collectively, we believe there is a great opportunity for a broad set of innovations in compute, GPU, networking, software and services to support core and edge AI infrastructure.
7. According to the 650 Group, the AI switching market is forecasted to exceed $10 billion in 2027.
8. Our scalable fabric for AI, coupled with the proven power-saving capabilities of Cisco Silicon One, put us in a strong position to build out the infrastructure needed for AI clusters, and we are laser-focused on winning in this space.
9. In our Collaboration portfolio, we recently introduced a range of truly game-changing AI capabilities spanning the entire Webex suite, as well as new devices for reimagined workspaces at our WebexOne event.
10. With the incremental multibillion-dollar AI infrastructure opportunity, the increasing criticality of security and observability and what we believe Cisco and Splunk can do together for our customers, is truly exciting.

### Scott Herren, Chief Financial Officer
11. In Q1, we also closed several acquisitions, all of which are highly complementary to our internal R&D, and in line with our strategy to strengthen our position in cloud, security, observability, and AI with targeted strategic M&A.
