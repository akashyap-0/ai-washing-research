## Prepared remarks

### Chuck Robbins, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer
1. We also announced several new capabilities across our security, collaboration, and observability portfolios, leveraging AI throughout.
2. We also continue to capitalize on the multibillion-dollar AI infrastructure opportunity.
3. This quarter, we announced the next phase in our partnership with NVIDIA to offer enterprises simplified cloud-based and on-prem AI infrastructure.
4. This includes both networking hardware and software to support advanced AI workloads. ⚑ context-dependent
5. We are clear beneficiaries of AI adoption, and this partnership further demonstrates the central role we play in AI and the overall technology ecosystem.
6. In web scale, we continue to see momentum with three of the top four customers deploying our hyperscale Ethernet AI fabric, leveraging Cisco-validated designs for AI infrastructure.
7. While there is tremendous opportunity ahead, we are still in the early stages of adoption of AI workloads.
8. AI is also becoming more pervasive across the Cisco Security Cloud.
9. For example, our new AI assistant and Secure Access lets customers create security access policies using natural language prompts reducing errors and speeding up policy administration by 70%.
10. In addition, we have now extended our AI-powered ThousandEyes into Cisco Secure Access joining past integrations with AppDynamics, WebEx Catalyst, and Meraki platforms.
11. Our continued generative AI innovations build upon our existing platform capabilities further enabling operations teams to focus on what matters most: minimizing tools for all, improving overall performance, and delivering highly secure digital experiences.
12. Going back to the pending Splunk acquisition, the combination of Splunk's complementary capabilities with ours and AI, security, and observability will create an end-to-end data platform to enhance our customers' digital resiliency.
